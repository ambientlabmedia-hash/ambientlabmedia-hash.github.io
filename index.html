Ambient Lab

Ambient Lab publishes original long-form ambient audio on YouTube: shaped noise, simulated rain, wind and ocean soundscapes, and slowly evolving drones for sleep, focus and quiet background listening.

All audio and visuals are generated procedurally by the channel's own software. No third-party music, samples or recordings are used.

About the Ambient Lab application

"Ambient Lab" is also the name of a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos and read the channel's own YouTube Analytics through the official YouTube APIs. It is not offered to other users.
