1. The Ambient Lab application (Google / YouTube data)

The Ambient Lab application is used exclusively by the channel owner with the owner's own Google account. With the owner's consent it accesses, via the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API:

  • upload and management of the channel's own videos, thumbnails and video metadata;
  • the channel's own aggregated analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, impressions, estimated revenue).

It does not access data of other users, does not collect personal data of viewers (YouTube Analytics data is aggregated), and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties. Data is stored only on the owner's own devices and used only to run and evaluate the owner's channel. Ambient Lab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored tokens and data are then deleted.
